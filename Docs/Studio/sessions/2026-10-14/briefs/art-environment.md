## The job
### A. The bench's look (briefs the Blender lead builds from)
1. **The silicone repair mat**: the real reference (phone-repair silicone mats: grid, rulers, moulded screw cups, magnetic zone — describe; no brand), its size on the bench (the Technical Director decides scale; give it relative to the bench top and the device), the **cool slate-blue** colour (hex, within the house palette's rules; why it reads against warm devices from the close-up at 58°), the grid and rulers as texture, **the moulded compartments**: how many screw cups and their size, the parts well, the old-parts bin at the edge (part of the mat or a small separate bin — pick), the fresh-part spot (how it's marked), and the tool stand that replaces the plain caddy block (where, what it looks like, how each tool sits, the cloth's place).
2. **The cloth** in three states: folded on its stand, open in the hand, bunched in a stroke (so the code can blend or swap them), real microfibre lens-cloth references.
3. **The camera's strap ("the handle … doesn't flow like how a camera would")**: real rangefinder straps (eyelet split rings, the strap's curve from the lug, the leather keeper, the drape onto the table), Grace's scratches kept (the scene calls it "KEEP - scratched sentimental strap"); and check the camera **body's** outline against a real rangefinder (Canonet QL17 / Olympus 35 SP: the top plate's step, the rounded ends) — say if it needs work.
4. **The clean shutter mechanism** that replaces today's single steel petal as the fresh part: a clean, bright copy of the jammed mechanism (same silhouette, no dirt, gears bright), so the swap reads as "old one out, new one in".
5. **Screws and holes in proportion** (with the Technical Director's sizes): what a real small Phillips head and its hole look like at the close-up's distance; the hole as a small threaded opening in a slight dip.
6. **Tool icons** for the checklist card (UI/UX's brief): orthographic renders of the five tools, a screw and a "new part", their style and size.
