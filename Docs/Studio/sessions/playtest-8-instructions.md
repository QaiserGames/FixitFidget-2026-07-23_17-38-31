# Playtest 8: how to run it (for Mansoor; 20 Oct 2026)

*Playtest 7's player felt "lost most of the time" and stopped. This build is the answer to that (`Docs/Studio/sessions/2026-10-20.md`): a "Now" line that always says the one next step, button icons instead of button names, bigger and bolder words, one way to choose a reply, amber for "next" at the bench, the HUD in one column top right, no closing tint. Playtest 8 asks one question: **can someone who isn't you finish Day 3 without help, and do they want Day 4?** The scope stays frozen until the answer is yes.*

## Before anyone plays (15 minutes, once)

1. **Commit and push first** (GitHub Desktop), so the build they play is saved.
2. **A mouse and a pad on the table.** No trackpad. If the player is a pad player, give them the pad from the start.
3. **Unity:** open the café scene (`AcesCafeLayoutPlaytest`), then **Fixit Fidget › Playtest › Fresh game 1 - Start a new game (keeps your save as a copy)**. **Fresh game 2** brings your own save back afterwards.
4. **Settings** (they survive "Fresh game"): the intro on **Full**, **Aim help** on (it is now on by default), **Vibration** on.
5. **Play the first two minutes of Day 1 yourself** and look at it fresh: the font, the icons, the top-right column, the amber outline at the bench. Anything that looks wrong to you, write down before the player sits.

## Record it (this time it matters most)

1. QuickTime Player › File › **New Screen Recording**; in **Options**, choose your **microphone**, so their voice is in it.
2. Record from the main menu to the end of Day 3, or until they stop wanting to play.
3. Save it in `~/Documents/FixitFidget/Recordings/` as `playtest8-<who>-<date>.mov` (that folder is never committed).

Without a recording we can only guess where they were lost; with one, every stall gets a timestamp and a cause.

## While they play

- **Nobody helps.** If they ask "what do I do?", say "what do you think?" and write down the time.
- Write three things with the time: **where they stopped and looked around**, **what they said out loud**, **when they smiled**.
- Let them stop whenever they want. Where they stop is the most useful thing we learn.

## What to watch (to notice, not to steer)

- **Lost?** Each time they stop and look around: what did the Now line (top left) say, and did they read it?
- **Reading:** do they read the Now line, the bench's cards and the night's notes without leaning in? Do the button icons match the pad in their hands?
- **Choosing:** any reply picked by accident, by day or at night?
- **The bench:** do they see the amber "next" outline and the mark where to set a device down? Does the cloth read as a cloth? Does the pocket watch reach **Perfect** (the cloth's wipe of the crystal asks for a flip first)?
- **The HUD:** do they notice **Last orders** (the honey sign and the bell) and **Closed** without being told? Do they know the goal is the franchise (the five stars, "Franchise at 5 stars")?
- **Night 1:** do they find the back door, the man at the bins, Barnaby, 12 West Street (the yellow house with the porch light), without help?
- **The phone at closing:** do they find the Shop and restock the beans?
- **The café:** does it feel lived in? Does anyone sit in the lounge by the window?
- **Anything that looks broken** (a part you can see through, two names on one person, text off its card): write the time.

## After each player (5 minutes)

Ask, and write their words, not yours:
1. What is this game about?
2. What did you have to do to win?
3. When were you lost?
4. **Do you want to play Day 4?**

Then fill in the playtest journal (the link from playtest 6) together.

## Afterwards

Tell the next session where the recordings are. It will timestamp every stall against the Now line's steps and bring you the list.
