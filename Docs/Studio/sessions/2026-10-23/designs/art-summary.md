## Patterns

- Soft things built from hard shapes look worst: the coats, the held cloth, Grace's coat and quilt, the tote, the pickup row's wraps, and the house kit's coat, shoes and cushion. A soft thing needs sag, folds and round corners, or it stays out.
- Plants made of balls or spikes clash with the café's leafy plants: the West Street trees, the patio flower, the car-park bushes, the door planters and the olive trees. One more Kenney swap would clear most of them.
- A few materials cause many items: the milky café glass, one-sided shades and backs, photo textures that dull flat paint (Grace's yellow, the plaster boards), and whites that glow (the patio marble, the ledge, the white coffee).
- Some props leave the palette: lived-in props in pure red and green or all black, and bought Synty and Kenney pieces at the door (boulders, chunky chairs, the magenta van) beside the café's own.
- Fix passes stopped at a group's edge and new cameras show what nobody built: the old tray walls, two ball trees, the lid's underside, blank walls and ramps, bare glowing balls, and thin rods that read as stray sticks.
- People look like one setup fault (a second body inside customers, lost heads, copied red jackets, cross faces), and the house kit and shop furniture need their pictures, shells and wood grain fixed before they're placed.

## How the list was made

- The same asset seen by several reviewers is one item, with the worst severity and all their pictures. Two different problems on one asset are two items: the desk's ruler and its colours, the pickup row's wraps and its controller, the patio table's marble and its brass, the pendant's see-through shade and its closed top, the phone's black tray walls and its cut ruler.
- Where reviewers disagreed, the item takes the more careful call (fix rather than keep), and its fix line names the other view: a23, a32, a41, a49, a61, a62.
- Confidence is the highest any reviewer gave that the problem is real.
- Some pictures come from the 13–15 Oct lab pack, from before the 20 Oct lounge and lamp changes. The reviewers checked those against 20 Oct photos where they could.
- In the model-sheet pictures, the side facing away from the light looks dark. That's the lighting, not the paint.
- Pictures with a catch: a79 (the café coat stand) has no photo, because it's been off since 9 Oct, so its picture is the empty waiting nook. a24's dark band on the mat is faint. a44's grey box was named from the desk model's plain back.
