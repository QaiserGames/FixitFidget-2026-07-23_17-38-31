## Patterns

- **Every weak model in this half is a soft thing, and every hard thing is good.**
  - The weak ones: the held cloth (a cone), Grace's coat and scarf (a capsule and two bars), her asleep quilt (a tray and a headless mound), her armchair's lace (a flat board, fine at its distance), and the reunion cups (a smooth tube).
  - It's the same family as the café's chair coats he named.
  - One short soft-goods Blender pass (cloth, coat, quilt head) clears the family, or removal where the thing adds nothing.
- **CF_Glass reads milky wherever it's near the camera**: the bench screen, the water station, the grinder. It's one material and one fix, made in the kit definition so Kit 1 doesn't undo it.
- **Single-sided shells seen from inside**: GH_Lampshade (three lamps, including R2's HK_Pendant) and CK_BanquetteBay's backrest. The sheets' face test finds them. Fix with Render Face Both on a lamp-only material, or a closing panel.
- **Grain versus flat wood**:
  - The café's grain comes from the kit's importer remaps (the CK_ pieces) or from a one-time scene step (everything else under the café).
  - Pieces spawned at runtime, such as bought furniture, get neither.
  - Grace's house is flat colour throughout. That's fine: it's another building, seen at night.
- **Placeholders that read empty**: GH_Photo, the cup sleeve's missing rims, the door panels. All are in Grace's house, and all are low priority.
- **Source mix**: none shows in this half. Every model is the game's own Blender work, except the CC0 "Beach" rig inside Customer, which is hidden: walk-ins wear POLYGON City bodies at runtime (CafeLife_..._031410__01, Dialogue_..._07).
