**Patterns**
- Every soft thing built from the kits' hard primitives fails: LI_Coat_OnChair, HK_Coat, HK_Cushion, HK_Shoes, HK_Slippers, LI_PickupRow's bags and wrapped lamp (and R1's GH_CoatStand coat, BT_ClothHeld, GH_Armchair's lace). The scripts build from boxes, lofts and ribbons of even thickness with straight edges; rigid things come out well, cloth doesn't. A soft thing needs sag (a hem that dips), at least three folds, pinched or rounded corners, and a warm mid-value colour that reads from above, or it stays out.
- The house kit (42 HK_ pieces) is placed by no step, so its 11 findings are S3 today. Before any of it goes into a house: the pendant's shade, the four paintings, the door's glass and the mug's floor.
- The kit builder's defaults: `fixit_blender.Prop.finish()` gives every mesh metre-scale box UVs and recalculates every normal. That is right for solid things in tiling materials and wrong for one-image textures (the paintings) and hand-flipped shells (the pendant). Pictures need 0–1 UVs (the LivedPiece way), and shells need a real thickness.
- Three furniture families meet once the shop sells furniture: the built-in CK_ kit (grain, chamfers, 780–1,460 triangles a seat or table), Tier 1 "Workshop" and Tier 2 "Marble & Brass". The T1/T2 FBXs carry flat materials (the sheets show T1 as flat copper); the scene's T1 copies (the High-top, the lounge table) get the room's grain only through `CafeFurnishing.Remap`, so whatever places bought furniture must call the same remap. The remap knows only CC_Wood_*: T2's marble, walnut and brass stay flat.
- Reading the sheets: the key light comes from the -Z side and above, so faces pointing +Z (every kit piece's front) and the BOTTOM views are dark by lighting, not paint. Colour was judged on the -Z, TOP and 3/4 views.
