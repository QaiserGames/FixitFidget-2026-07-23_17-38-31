- **Every soft thing is a solid.** The coats are slabs, the tote is a faceted box, the held cloth is a blob, and the condiment station and donor parts are blocks. The lived-in pass and the bench built soft goods from boxes. Once the coats go, the tote and the cloth are next in line.
