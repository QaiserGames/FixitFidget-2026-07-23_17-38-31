**Bench 8, the clumsy hand.** Sixteen runs: the camera's Day 1 clean and Day 2 swap, the phone's screen, the watch's seized movement; mouse and pad; 60 and 15 fps.

- With the **mouse** it was already right: Grace's clean 10–12 s (bar 45), her swap 8–9 s (bar 30), the phone 33–38 s (75), the watch 45–53 s (110); every run Perfect; nothing lost.
- With a **pad** the first run broke: the camera's film path took **161 s**, "wasn't clean after 15 s of scrubbing" nine times over. Two causes, both real for a player with a controller. (1) The aim help fought the scrub: every turn of a stick's stroke passes through the dead zone, the snap glided the cursor back onto the spot's middle, and over the spot the friction halved the stroke — a 6 px scrub. (2) A stick's stroke is a line through the middle; the brush reaches 0.75 of the spot's radius, so the edges were never touched and the spot stalled below the 90 % finisher. **Fixed:** no aim help while the cloth or brush is down on dirt (`ItemInspector.OfferAimHelp`); a pad's rub reaches 1.35× (the reach its in-place orbit already had: `BenchRules.PadRubReach`); and **the snap never undoes a push** (`PadCursor.Nearest`: at 15 fps, a nudge back toward the screwdriver was pulled back to the point it left, fourteen times, 8 s, and the tool was never taken). The camera's clean with a pad: **161 s → 11.6 s**.
- **The cloth now shows what it cleans** (call 23): the wad sits 4 mm lower (`ToolPickup.FarEdgeUnderTip`), and the hover card fades while you rub (`HoverTooltipUI`): Grace's lens in sight over the wipe 26–30 % → **77–83 %** (bar 40 %).
- **The watch's cover no longer says "Take them out first" while the screws go back in** (`RemovablePart.Prompt`).
- Three of the lab's own misreadings fixed: a screw fetched back over its hole counted as hidden (its shank in the hole); the pad hand's waggle was a sine (it swung to one side) and the same size on every spot (it now sizes its stroke to the spot, as the mouse hand does).
- **The re-run: 149/154, every run Perfect within its bar, 0 lost, 0 hidden.** The five left are the pad's strokes per spot (median 3–4 against the target's 3; the lens with a pad took 12 strokes, about 3 s) and the pad's 60/15 fps parity on three steps (the hand's own corrective pushes at 15 fps: the game's cursor is frame-rate independent).
