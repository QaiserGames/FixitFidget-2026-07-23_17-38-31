## Hard rules
- **No new mechanics** (Bible §2): no new verb, resource, meter or loop. Fix, tune, clarify, polish. Say it in your report if anything you did brushes this.
- **Locks**: Grace's Day 1 latte suppression is intentional; the overhead is isometric and the hands-on views are first-person; the bench is scripted (no physics in the core loop); the HUD carries numbers and the world carries colour and shape; the circuit/human-fault locks; saves at Open Tomorrow.
- **Unity 6.5**: `Object.GetInstanceID()` does not compile (use `GetEntityId()` or names). Use `Object.FindObjectsByType<T>(FindObjectsInactive.X)` / `FindObjectsByType<T>()` without a `FindObjectsSortMode` argument (that overload is deprecated). `Object.FindAnyObjectByType<T>()` is fine.
- **The code's voice**: comments explain WHY in plain words and quote her or his note where a change answers one (look at the header comments of the files you change). When you change behaviour, update the file's header comment so it stays true. Dialogue/notes: aim for 90 characters, never over 150; one line per bubble; a note fits `WordBudget` (12 words) where the code enforces it.
- **Button names are icons from today** (builder T's contract, which every builder writes against): `PadInput.Label(PadButton)` and every `ControlHints` verb return a TextMesh Pro sprite tag for the active device (e.g. `<sprite name="ps_cross">`, `<sprite name="key_e">`), not a word. `PadInput.Word(PadButton)` / `ControlHints.Word(...)` give the old plain word for logs and labs. So: never wrap a label in "[ ]" (an icon needs no brackets) and never write "Press X" with a button's name spelled out; write `ControlHints.Interact + " Talk to Grace"`-style text. Labs must compare against the same helpers, never against literal button words.
- **Readable text** (her: "your text needs to be bolder"; the day and time "way too small"; "flip it over… it's just so tiny"): anything a player reads is at least 24 px at the 1920 x 1080 reference (body), 20 px only for secondary labels, and the most important line on a screen 28–34 px; bold or semibold for anything that instructs; text over the world always sits on a plate (UiSkin.Band) or carries the skin's shadow (UiSkin.Shadowed).
- **Labs**: if your change alters something a lab asserts (a string, a size, a colour, a position), update that lab in the same change if you own it; otherwise list the exact edit in your report. Never weaken a check to make it pass; change what it expects only when the behaviour change is intended, and say so.
- Keep every public member that other files or labs use (grep before renaming or removing anything).
