## The design (the plan he was sent; make the visual calls well and list them)
- **One column at the top right** (1920 x 1080 reference; right margin 28, top 24), each element on a soft dark plate (UiSkin.Band, radius 12) or one plate behind the column, so it reads over any background (the reviewers: "$0 TODAY" white on white tile; the clock white on the counter):
  1. **The hanging sign**, moved from the bottom left (keep its art, sway and flip): OPEN in the brand green; LAST ORDERS on a honey-amber face; CLOSED on a warm brick/plum face (not alarm red). Sub-line 20 px ("Come in", "Closing at 8 PM", "See you tomorrow").
  2. **The day row:** a small sun dial — a 96 x 48 px half-arc track (paper, 4 px) with an 18 px gold sun travelling from its left end (opening) to its right end (closing); the arc warms from pale gold to honey/orange in the last hour; at closing the sun sets below the right end and a small moon shows. Beside it the day ("Mon · Day 1", 22 px semibold) and the time ("9:30 AM", 30 px bold). This replaces the green bar.
  3. **The takings:** the bill/coin icon right against the number (8 px gap), "$142" 40 px bold, "today" 18 px. The "+$6" chip pops just under the figure and rises into it as it fades (keep the jolt, count-up and punch).
  4. **The stars:** the café's stars (ReputationLedger's StarsEarned; ReputationRules.MaxStars) as five small stars (draw them as Images/sprites: the UI font may lack ★) and "Franchise at 5 stars" 18 px under them.
- **No screen tint.** Remove the full-screen edge glow entirely (the amber pulse at last orders and the red bloom at closing, HudCorners ~546-560 and the vignette). The last hour is told by: the sign flipping to LAST ORDERS with the existing soft chime (`hud.lastorders` in SoundBank) and a faster sway; the dial warming; and the light through the windows turning golden (CafeDaylight already warms from about 16:24 on the in-game clock: read it; if the change is too subtle, push its last-hour warmth a little — no new lights). Closing: the sign flips to CLOSED with a small ding (an existing cue; read SoundBank) and a 0.5 s scale punch.
- **Fix:** at closing the sign must never show OPEN during its turn (the reviewers saw "OPEN · COME IN" at 8:00 PM, Pause__pause/30 and CafeLife 06-closing-time): set the faces before the turn starts.
- **Stock chips** (bottom right, as now): 22 px bold, and they say when to buy: "Out of beans · restock at closing", "Low on cups · 7 left · restock at closing" (keep within WordBudget's 12-word alert budget).
- **Quiet at the bench** (the existing behaviour): adapt it to the new column; the bench card (builder B's BenchCards) sits top right under the takings: expose the column's bottom edge in reference pixels (`public float TopRightBottom`) and report the one-line edit BenchCards needs to sit under it.
- **Keep the public API** that labs read (Showing, BottomShowing, TakingsShown, TakingsTarget, TakingsPunch, EdgesShown, BillShowing, SignWord, LastOrders, SignClosed, ClockLine, ClockGap, ClockLineSize, CupsChipShowing, BeansChipShowing, ChipsText, CashRect, SignRect, ChipsRect, QuietAlpha…), keeping each member's meaning (say how you mapped any that moved: e.g. BottomShowing now means the chips).
