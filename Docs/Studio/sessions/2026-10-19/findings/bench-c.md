## Patterns
- Prompts are words, not icons, and the words are Xbox's (A/B/X/Y/View) in all 36 pad photos; "X" means a different button on her pad.
- All bench text is 15-17 px at 1080p and lives in the two right-hand corners, away from the device in the centre.
- Names and marks lie: new parts inherit the fault word ("New cracked screen"), and the card shows a check on "Cover on" before it is done.
- The next-thing outline is thin, blinks or is missing (Screws back, new part on the pad); it is the only "where" the game gives.
- Tools drop where released and stay there: on the device, on the cups, across the well; only the cloth goes home.

## Looks right
- The job card system itself: customer's words, one icon per step, the current step named below, old part to the tray, new part on the pad, "Fixed! · Perfect" with sparkles, then "Pick it up". Keep it; make it bigger.
- Tooltip second lines ("Needs the brush", "The cover goes back on first") are good plain hints; keep the wording.
- Models hold up in close-up: pocket-watch dial and red jewels, phone internals with the charging coil, cracked-screen cracks, screws with real heads in the cups. Scale between device, tools and cups is believable.
- UI skin is consistent: cream cards, dark panels, one green, rounded corners; the slate mat and its ruler are by design.
- Not bugs, lab timing: the cover/screen caught mid-flight to the well/tray and looking oversized (2m60/2p60/3m60-step02-Pryoff, 2m60-step03-Liftout), the "Fixed!" banner caught tiny while popping in (0m15-end, 3m60-end), the card icon caught mid-grow (3p60-step06-Coveron), the "CAFÉ LAB" banner.
