## What went wrong, for next time

- **Fable 5.1 usage ran out** four minutes into the session. Five agents died and were rerun on Opus 5.5. Then the session limit stopped four agents mid-work (they resumed after the reset), and the container restarted once. Lesson: fewer agents at once, and outputs written as they go.
- **Unity can't be driven while the Mac's screen is locked.**
- **Unity compiles a changed script only while it's the front app.** A lab also freezes the moment another window is in front, because the project's Run In Background is off. The Day 1 guide lab sat on its first photo for 14 minutes until Unity came back to the front. Lesson: before a lab run, take the screen (with his OK) or ask him to keep Unity in front. A reimport followed by entering Play Mode also forces the compile.
- **The slip.** Claude's Stop after the Hands on it lab came after the lab had already left Play Mode by itself. So it started his game on his own save, Day 2, for about 30 seconds before Claude stopped it. The save is written only at the recap and when the next day starts (SaveManager.TrySaveRecap / TrySaveNextDay), so nothing was written. No day log changed either. Lesson: read the play state before every Stop, never after.
