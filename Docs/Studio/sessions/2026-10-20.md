## The review that looks at everything

Three layers, all in the repo, all read-only:

**Review 1, model sheets** (`Fixit Fidget › Review › Model sheets 1–4`). Unity photographs a model from 8 sides and tests every part's faces from 14 directions: inside-out, see-through from some side, transparent, missing material or mesh.
- *Model sheets 1, the devices and the bench tools* (the ones the player turns in their hands): found the strap on its first run (6 leather pieces see-through from above: the Blender script built them facing the mat; fixed in `hero_camera.py`, rebuilt, reimported). Tonight: **15 models, 76 parts, no S1, no S2.** It also called the watch's crystal smudge "no mesh, empty material" (S1 ×2): a dirt patch the game builds when it starts (GrimeSpot). A review that cries wolf stops being read, so it now says "built while playing" (S3).
- *Model sheets 4, every piece in the café scene* (first run tonight): 265 groups, 1,921 parts, 393 flags. Triaged by hand, with the flag pictures: **nothing a player sees is see-through.** The S1s are the undersides of street props (manholes, road patches, parking meters: never seen from below), glass (meant to be clear), the café's inside-only ceiling and Grace's house's inside walls (the cut-away, by design), Synty office blocks' open tops (each capped by the next piece), distant ground seen edge-on, empty "doorway" meshes (nothing to draw), and the Kenney plants (switched off in the scene). Its limits are written up in the tool: it judges each group alone from outside, so it can't tell the cut-away from a hole. The next step for this layer is a pass **from the game's own cameras** (overhead, first person, the night), so only what a player can see is judged.

**Review 2, every lab photo checks its own words** (`ReviewScreen`, `Fixit Fidget › Review › Screens 1`). Each lab photo measures every word on the screen (size at 1080p, contrast against what's behind it, weight, a button written as a word), boxes the problems, and Screens 1 gathers them into one ranked report. Its first full gather tonight: 543 photos, 93 texts at S1. Two-thirds weren't the UI's fault, so the gather now:
- keeps **the newest run of each lab** (a lab run before and after a fix is one lab; Screens 3 keeps the history);
- lists **words in the world** (street names, house numbers, the drinks machine's paddles) on their own, by the largest they were seen: their size is the camera's distance, not a font size;
- skips a list's rows **scrolled out of its window**.

What it caught that was real, fixed tonight:
- **The phone's coloured words were faint on their pale chips**: orange on peach 2.9:1 ("Low on beans", "+2 reputation", "Let down"), the grey of a tab that isn't open 2.7:1, green on mint 4.2, red on pink 3.9, white on the orange price 3.6. The chips keep their colours; the words are deeper (each ≥ 4.5:1), the price pills a deeper orange, the section headings 16 → 18 px.
- **The sign's "Come in"** was the brand green at 85 % on paper, 3.5:1: deep green now, 7.3:1.
- **"Valerie" twice over the till** (seen in the Day 1 lab's photo): when the name of what E acts on hangs below a customer to keep clear of Ace's head, Day 1's green pin over the same customer didn't give way. The commonest moment of Day 1. Fixed in `UiPins` (the name says who it's about, wherever it's drawn), and the Day 1-3 labs now count every look of the day for two pins naming the same person.

What it still flags that is a call, not a bug (left for him; most of the 49 S1s): the phone's tab labels, its small all-caps kickers, its review chips and badge digits are 16 px (the phone's width; the builder kept them on purpose), the ticket's patience word 16 px, the HUD's "today" and "Franchise at 5 stars" 20 px (XAG asks 26); the open tab's label is the brand orange, 3.6:1.
